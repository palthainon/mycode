why this exists

Most "free" practice-test sites gate the good questions behind a signup, a subscription, or a countdown timer designed to make you panic-buy. These don't. Pick an exam, pick the domains you want to work on, and practice. Your progress stays in your own browser.

Every question is original, written against the vendor's public exam objectives, with an explanation for every choice. They are not real exam questions or dumps. We don't claim to match today's live exam exactly; we aim to teach the material it covers.
