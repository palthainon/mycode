Maintenance Window

The change is staged, the rollback is written, and now you wait.

what's in this section

Small games and ambient scenes for the quiet part of a change window: waiting on a reboot, a replication, or a vendor bridge that will reconvene "in about ten minutes." Everything runs in your browser and works offline. Music is generated live with the Web Audio API. Nothing streams, and nothing downloads.

Lo-fi Wizard

An ASCII night shift. A wizard types through his change plan, sips coffee, pets his cat and checks the blinking racks behind him. Put it full screen and tune the radio to lo-fi, a fantasy tavern, an enchanted forest or 8-bit chiptune.

Start the shift

Spellcheck

An 8-bit typing game. Bugs, phishing emails, jammed printers, 404 ghosts and ransomware march on the wizard, each carrying an IT word. Type it to cast. Every fifth wave a legacy mainframe shows up. Tracks your WPM and accuracy.

Defend the datacenter

Datacenter Quest

A text adventure. PROD-ORACLE-01 is down and the KVM is dead, so the on-call wizard has to walk there. Get past guardians who quiz you on ports, subnets, chmod, cron, RAID, routing and IPv6. Type commands like it's 1980, or play with buttons.
