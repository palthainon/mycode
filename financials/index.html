Financial Tools

Loan, mortgage, and savings calculators for financial planning and investment analysis

what's in this category

Calculators for the financial decisions that show up after a job change, a mortgage shopping trip, or a year-end retirement review. Everything is browser-side math — no inputs sent to a server, no account required, no upsell to a "premium" version that just rebrands the same formulas. Designed for people who'd rather understand the underlying numbers than trust a black box.

The Take-Home Pay Calculator estimates net pay after US federal income tax, FICA, state tax, 401(k), and HSA contributions. Amortization generates a full month-by-month schedule for any fixed-rate loan, including principal/interest breakdown. Compound Interest projects investment growth across contribution and return scenarios. Debt Payoff Comparison runs snowball versus avalanche side by side so you can pick the strategy that fits. Retirement Calculator models long-term savings with adjustable contribution, return, and inflation assumptions. Salary to Hourly Converter translates between annual, monthly, biweekly, weekly, and hourly rates.

your numbers stay on your machine

None of these tools store, transmit, or analyze your financial inputs. Run a paycheck scenario or stress-test a mortgage on a public coffee-shop laptop without worrying about who else might see it. If you want to bookmark a specific scenario, copy the URL — most calculators encode their state in the address bar.
