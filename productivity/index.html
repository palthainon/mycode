Productivity Tools

Simple, effective tools for time management and focused work

what's in this category

Small, focused utilities for staying on task and coordinating across time zones. No notifications you didn't ask for, no streaks to maintain, no AI suggesting you "optimize your workflow." Just a Pomodoro timer that beeps when the interval is up, a scratchpad that saves what you type, and a meeting planner that respects everyone's working hours.

The Pomodoro Timer runs configurable work/break intervals with audible cues. Plaintext Scratchpad is a persistent textarea that autosaves to your browser — perfect for capturing stray thoughts during a meeting without opening a "real" notes app. Timezone Meeting Planner lays out business-hours overlap across multiple locations, including a polar globe view, so you can find a workable meeting time at a glance.

Pomodoro Timer

A customizable Pomodoro timer with circular countdown display, browser notifications, and audio alerts. Track your focus sessions with built-in history and statistics. Supports work sessions, short breaks, and long breaks.

Plaintext Scratchpad

A persistent scratchpad that auto-saves every keystroke to your browser. Open it, jot anything, close the tab — your text is still there when you come back. Word/character counts and one-click download as .txt.

Timezone Meeting Planner

Pick a time in your home timezone and instantly see what it looks like everywhere else. Business-hours indicators (green/yellow/red) make it obvious when you've found a workable overlap.
