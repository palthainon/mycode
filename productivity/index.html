what's in this category

Small, focused utilities for staying on task and coordinating across time zones. No notifications you didn't ask for, no streaks to maintain, no AI suggesting you "optimize your workflow." Just a Pomodoro timer that beeps when the interval is up, a scratchpad that saves what you type, and a meeting planner that respects everyone's working hours.

The Pomodoro Timer runs configurable work/break intervals with audible cues. Plaintext Scratchpad is a persistent textarea that autosaves to your browser — perfect for capturing stray thoughts during a meeting without opening a "real" notes app. Timezone Meeting Planner lays out business-hours overlap across multiple locations, including a polar globe view, so you can find a workable meeting time at a glance.

free & open source PDF tools

Edit, annotate, and organize PDFs with community-built alternatives to Adobe Acrobat. These are external projects: use the official links below to try them, download them, or help improve their source code.

For existing text edits, start with LibreOffice Draw or BentoPDF. For handwritten notes, use Xournal++; for moving pages around, use PDF Arranger. PDFCraft and PdfCraft are two separate projects with similar names.

PDFCraft (browser toolkit)

Merge, split, compress, convert, fill forms, and add text, images, or annotations. The project processes documents locally in your browser.

Browser · AGPL-3.0

Use PDFCraft · PDFCraft source & contribute

PdfCraft (ArtCraft)

A native PDF workbench for reading, organizing pages, combining documents, comments, and forms. Still in early development; editing existing text remains limited.

Windows, macOS, Linux · MIT / Apache-2.0

Get PdfCraft · PdfCraft source & contribute

BentoPDF

Edit text, annotate, sign, convert, and organize PDFs in your browser. Document processing runs locally; no signup is required.

Browser · AGPL-3.0; commercial licensing also available

Use BentoPDF · BentoPDF source & contribute

LibreOffice Draw

Open a PDF in Draw to edit existing text and graphics, then export it back to PDF. Best for small corrections; complex layouts and fonts may shift during import.

Windows, macOS, Linux · MPL-2.0

Get LibreOffice · Contribute to LibreOffice

PDF Arranger

Visually merge, split, rotate, crop, and reorder pages on your computer. A focused page organizer rather than an editor for existing text.

Windows, Linux · GPL-3.0

Get PDF Arranger · PDF Arranger source & contribute

Xournal++

Add handwritten notes, highlights, drawings, and text over PDF pages, then export to PDF. Works well with a pen tablet; it does not rewrite the original PDF text.

Windows, macOS, Linux · GPL-2.0

Get Xournal++ · Xournal++ source & contribute
